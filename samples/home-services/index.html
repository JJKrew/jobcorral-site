The missed call, in writing

Whether it’s an AC that’s down or a pipe that’s leaking, a caller who hits voicemail usually tries the next company. On a live Job Corral setup, the shop texts back in seconds.

Step 1

A call is missed

The tech is on a roof, under a sink, or the office is already closed.

Step 2

A text goes out

The caller gets a message from Cinderlane Mechanical asking what’s going on.

Step 3

The shop calls back

The details are in writing, so the callback happens before the customer moves on.

If a call is missed, the caller gets a text.

On a live Job Corral setup, a missed call doesn’t go to voicemail and die there. The caller gets a text from the shop in seconds, and the shop gets the job details to call back.

  1. A homeowner calls while the tech is on a roof or in an attic.
  2. They get a text from Inkwood Heating & Air: “Sorry we missed you — what’s going on?”
  3. The shop sees the reply and calls back first, before the next name on Google.
